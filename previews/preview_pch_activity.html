About the Book

The Pownalborough Court House Activity Book introduces young readers and visitors to the people, objects, skills, and stories connected with the historic courthouse in Dresden, Maine.

Learning Through Activities

The book combines history with puzzles, matching activities, observation challenges, family history, period tools, foodways, textile production, and other activities designed to encourage children to look closely, ask questions, and explore how people lived in the past.

Historical Setting

Activities draw on the history of the Pownalborough Court House and the families who lived and worked there, using the building and its surviving objects as starting points for learning about everyday life in eighteenth- and nineteenth-century Maine.
