About the Book

The Pownalborough Court House Coloring Book introduces young readers to the historic courthouse in Dresden, Maine, through illustrations based on the building, its collections, and the people and activities associated with it.

Coloring History

Each page offers a visual entry point into the past, from the architecture of the courthouse to household objects, clothing, tools, and scenes of daily life. The illustrations are designed to encourage close observation while giving children a creative way to engage with local history.

Historical Setting

The book draws on the history of the Pownalborough Court House and the families who lived and worked there, using surviving objects and documented details as inspiration for scenes from eighteenth- and nineteenth-century Maine.
