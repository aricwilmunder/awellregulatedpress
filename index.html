A Well-Regulated Press publishes historically grounded work developed through research, preservation, interpretation, education, community collaboration, and outreach.

Beginning with artifacts, archival records, historic places, and the people connected to them, we create books, research, educational resources, and public-history projects that make the past accessible to readers, students, educators, museums, researchers, and the public.

Our Initiatives

Our initiatives bring research, preservation, interpretation, education, community collaboration, and public outreach together around a shared historical subject.

Each initiative can develop in different ways, from original research and publications to educational resources, museum collaborations, websites, and public presentations.
